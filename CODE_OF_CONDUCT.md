# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the libapta-audio and APTA
community respectful, professional and free from harassment for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background or personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- communicate constructively and focus criticism on technical content;
- respect differing experience levels, platforms and implementation choices;
- provide reproducible evidence for technical claims where practical;
- accept maintainer decisions about scope, compatibility and release safety;
- protect private, security-sensitive and personally identifying information;
- give attribution and respect licenses and intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, sustained disruption, deliberate misinformation, publishing
private information without permission, sexualized conduct, or retaliation
against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, reviews or other
contributions that violate this policy. Responses may include a private warning,
temporary participation limits or a permanent ban, depending on severity and
pattern.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an available
private GitHub contact channel. Security vulnerabilities must instead follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as the
platform permits. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly
involved in a report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project in public or private project-related communication.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, and is adapted
to this project's maintainer and reporting structure.
